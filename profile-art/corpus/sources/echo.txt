A minimalist text art studio where users paste prose and transform it into interactive word-based visualizations. Saved pieces persist in a local SQLite database through Echo's Express API.
Words are humanity's primary mechanism for understanding one another. Language carries emotion, memory, identity, intention. Yet modern communication increasingly prioritizes speed over reflection — compressing meaning into fragments, captions, messages, and signals. In trying to communicate faster, we often lose the emotional weight that language once carried.
Echo was created as a response to that loss.
Echo transforms writing into living visual systems that continue evolving after the sentence itself ends. Instead of treating text as static information, Echo interprets language as movement: words cluster, drift, repeat, orbit, fragment, and reform over time.
Each form gives language a different kind of afterlife.
Maps relationships between connected ideas and recurring language patterns.
Disperses words into atmospheric fields that move like memory.
Compresses language into typographic residue and monochrome traces.
Pulls repeated language into motion, recurrence, and collapse.
Organizes words around semantic anchors and emotional weight.
